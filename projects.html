TradeLab AI

trade-lab-ai.vercel.app

A unified backtesting and strategy platform for forex, stocks, and crypto. Pulls live candle data from Binance, lets you build and save trading strategies, and backtests them — including strategies described in plain English and converted to code by AI.

  • Multi-asset live OHLCV candle data from Binance, any symbol/timeframe (1m–1d)
  • TradingView-style interactive charting with symbol switching
  • Manual strategy builder — EMA Crossover, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands with custom risk settings
  • AI Strategy Builder — plain-English strategy → runnable Python via a free LLM (OpenRouter)
  • Local-first strategy storage in the browser, nothing sent until you backtest
  • Backtest engine reporting win rate, total return, max drawdown, profit factor
